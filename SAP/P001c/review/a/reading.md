## Definitions and Mathlib meanings

Write Pμ(A) = (μ(A)).toReal, Eμ Z = ∫ Z dμ, Varμ Z for Mathlib's
`ProbabilityTheory.variance`, and Covμ(X,Y) for its `covariance`.
All event probabilities used in the theorems are under probability measures, so they
are finite. For a possibly nonmeasurable set μ(A) is the measure's outer evaluation.
The Bochner integral is totalized to zero for a nonintegrable function. Mathlib defines
Varμ Z as the real conversion of ∫⁻ |Z−Eμ Z|² dμ; in particular an infinite
extended variance would convert to zero. In these statements square integrability is
assumed or follows from specified distributions wherever ordinary moments are used.
Covμ(X,Y) = ∫ (X−Eμ X)(Y−Eμ Y) dμ.

`MemLp Z 2 μ` means that Z is almost everywhere strongly measurable and has finite
second absolute moment. It does not require pointwise measurability. `Measurable Z`
means measurability on the given sigma algebra. Equality `=ᵐ[μ]` is equality outside
a μ-null set. `IsProbabilityMeasure μ` means μ(Ω)=1.
`IdentDistrib X Y μ ν` includes almost everywhere measurability of both functions
and equality of their pushforward measures. `iIndepFun` is mutual independence of
the sigma algebras generated by the functions (all finite subfamilies), not merely
pairwise independence. `IndepFun` is the corresponding two-function property.
The functions in all pushforwards here are measurable or almost everywhere measurable.
Mathlib's otherwise-arbitrary pushforward convention is therefore inapplicable.

`gaussianReal m v`, with v a finite nonnegative real, is the normal law with mean m
and **variance** v. For v=0 it is the Dirac mass at m. For v>0 it has the usual normal
density. `withDensity` integrates its nonnegative extended-real density against the
base measure. `ENNReal.ofReal r` clips negative r to zero, but the displayed densities
are nonnegative on the specified parameter ranges.

The SAP definitions in Parts 1 and 2 expand as follows:

* `convergesInProbability μ Z c`: for every real ε>0,
  Pμ{dist(Zₙ,c)≥ε}→0 as n→∞. For real variables the distance is |Zₙ−c|.
* `convergesInDistribution μ Z ν`: for every real x at which
  t↦Pν((−∞,t]) is continuous, Pμ(Zₙ≤x)→Pν((−∞,x]).
* `isIID μ X`: every Xᵢ is measurable, the entire sequence is mutually independent,
  and each Xᵢ has the same law as X₀.
* `sampleAverage X n` = n⁻¹∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ⁻¹ Xᵢ. At n=0 the real division convention makes
  this zero. `empiricalLaw X n ω` = (n:ℝ≥0∞)⁻¹∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ⁻¹δ(Xᵢ(ω)); at zero this
  is the zero measure. A probability law is asserted only for n>0.
* `pluginVariance X n` = n⁻¹∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ⁻¹Xᵢ² − (n⁻¹∑ᵢ₌₀ⁿ⁻¹Xᵢ)².

  space, integrability of Y and m∘X, and, for every measurable set A in that state

The functional definitions in Part 3 expand as follows:

* `hadamardDerivative D H T F L`, in a real normed vector space E with continuous
  linear map L:E→ℝ, means: for every h∈H, every sequence tₙ of positive reals,
  and every sequence hₙ∈E, if tₙ→0, hₙ→h in norm, and F+tₙhₙ∈D for every n,
  then [T(F+tₙhₙ)−T(F)]/tₙ→L(h).
* `supNorm h` = supₓ|h(x)|, formed as a real `sSup`. All functions and differences
  on which this supremum is used in the differentiability predicate are bounded.
* `isCDF F` requires 0≤F≤1 everywhere, monotonicity, right continuity at each real
  x (limit along [x,∞)), F(x)→0 at −∞ and F(x)→1 at +∞.
* `cdfHadamard T F` asserts existence of L:(ℝ→ℝ)→ℝ with, in order:
  additivity on all functions; real homogeneity on all functions; existence of C≥0
  with |Lh|≤C supNorm(h) for every bounded h; and the following sequential property.
  For every bounded h, every positive sequence tₙ, and every sequence of bounded
  functions hₙ, if tₙ→0, supNorm(hₙ−h)→0 and every F+tₙhₙ is a CDF, then
  [T(F+tₙhₙ)−T(F)]/tₙ→Lh. The definition itself does not assume F is a CDF.
* `CDFSpace` is Mathlib's lp(ℝ-indexed copies of ℝ,∞): the space of all bounded
  real functions on ℝ, with the pointwise vector operations and uniform norm.
  There is no quotient by almost everywhere equality and no measurability or
  continuity requirement on its members. `Memℓp f ∞` is exactly boundedness of |f|.
* `embedCDF f` is f regarded as a member of that space if f is bounded, and the
  zero function otherwise. CDFs and the zero empirical CDF at n=0 are bounded.
  `cdfSet` is the subset of actual CDFs. `empiricalCDF X n ω` is the embedded
  CDF x↦P(empiricalLaw X n ω)((−∞,x]).
* `cdfTangent F` consists of bounded h such that, for each ε>0, some δ>0 makes
  |F(x)−F(y)|<δ imply |h(x)−h(y)|<ε for every real x,y, and such that h(x)=0
  whenever F(x)=0 or F(x)=1.
* `isCDFBridge F ν` requires ν((cdfTangent F)ᶜ)=0 and, for every natural r and
  every x,a:Fin r→ℝ, on putting
  v=∑ⱼ∑ₖ aⱼ[min(F(xⱼ),F(xₖ))−F(xⱼ)F(xₖ)]aₖ,
  requires v≥0 and the pushforward of ν by h↦∑ⱼaⱼh(xⱼ) to equal N(0,max(v,0)).
  A probability-measure assumption is separate in T57.
* `upperExpectation μ Z` is the real infimum of Eμ g over all measurable,
  integrable real g with Z(ω)≤g(ω) for every ω. For the bounded tests and
  probability measures in use, this set is nonempty and bounded below.
* `outerWeakConvergence μ Z ν` means: for every continuous f:E→ℝ and every
  proof that f is globally bounded (∃C ∀x |f(x)|≤C), both
  upperExpectationμ(f∘Zₙ)→Eν f and −upperExpectationμ(−f∘Zₙ)→Eν f.
  It does not assume measurability of Zₙ as an E-valued map.
* `quadraticFunctional G` = (G(0)−1/2)².

## SAP.P001c.T57
