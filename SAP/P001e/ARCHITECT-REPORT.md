The distinction is substantive. In the old uniform diagonal model, Y_t equals t almost surely at each fixed t, but Y_D is zero everywhere. Cylinder independence can preserve those coordinate laws. An independent measurable state with joint evaluation would instead allow the random-dose integral to be evaluated against the product law, giving E[Y_D]=E[D]=1/2. Thus the diagnostic does not supply the state contract needed by the evaluation-ready declaration. This check uses the exact hypotheses of [DecisionT91.lean](/home/ubuntu/sap/wt/ARCH-P001e/AronowAudit/P001/Wave1/Gap/DecisionT91.lean:266); no new separation proof was added.

The local canonical scope expressly says that the evaluation-ready contract is “not an equivalence or a redefinition” of the weaker process-only claim ([work.json](/home/ubuntu/sap/wt/ARCH-P001e/work.json:1044)). Moreover the existing declarations require [all-real-dose moments and injective encoding](/home/ubuntu/sap/wt/ARCH-P001e/AronowAudit/P001/Wave1/Gap/DecisionT91.lean:272); these requirements cannot be dropped by simply instantiating them. Clause (a) likewise retains its all-real-dose measurability, moments and process independence. The intermediate latent conditional mean given both X and D, a separate support-wide statement and an explicit comparison of two models with equal observable laws are not exported. These limitations require `restricted` under clause completeness, even though both useful identification components are retained.
