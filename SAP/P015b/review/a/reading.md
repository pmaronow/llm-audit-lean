## Common notation, with definitions expanded

All coefficients, outcomes, matrices and finite probabilities below are real. A `Fin n` index has exactly n elements, numbered 0 through n−1. Matrix multiplication, transposition, matrix-vector multiplication and dot products are the usual finite sums. Matrix `1` means the identity matrix. `M.PosSemidef` means that M is symmetric and vᵀMv≥0 for every vector v (Mathlib `LinearAlgebra/Matrix/PosDef.lean:60`).

Write `OLS(W,y)=(WᵀW)⁻¹Wᵀy`. This is exactly `sampleCoefficients`, without any additional rank premise in the definition. Mathlib's matrix inverse is the ordinary inverse when the determinant is nonzero and is the zero matrix otherwise (`LinearAlgebra/Matrix/NonsingularInverse.lean:166`). Thus rank assumptions must be checked where this definition is used. For a finite mass function p, write EₚZ=ΣₐpₐZₐ and p(E)=Σₐ1_E(a)pₐ. `IsFiniteLaw p` means pₐ≥0 for every a and Σₐpₐ=1. The finite conditional mean and probability at x are Eₚ[1_{X=x}Z]/p(X=x) and p(X=x,D=d)/p(X=x), respectively. Real division is total, with division by zero equal to zero. The finite residual weight is wₐ=(Dₐ−Eₚ[D|X=Xₐ])², and the effective masses are pₐwₐ/Eₚw. `finiteUnconfounded p X U D` means, for every x,u,d,

    p(X=x,U=u,D=d) p(X=x) = p(X=x,U=u) p(X=x,D=d).

On positive cells of a finite probability law this is conditional independence U ⟂ D | X. The definition itself does not separately require a probability law. `populationCoefficients p W Y` is (Eₚ[WWᵀ])⁻¹Eₚ[WY], with W here regarded as the column regressor vector of a row.

For a probability measure P, E denotes its real Bochner integral. `IsProbabilityMeasure P` means P(Ω)=1. `Integrable Z P` means almost-everywhere strong measurability and finite integral of |Z|; `MemLp Z 2 P` gives a finite second moment and almost-everywhere measurability. Bochner integrals of nonintegrable real functions are defined to be zero (`MeasureTheory/Integral/Bochner/Basic.lean:158`). Conditional expectations are Mathlib `condExp`: they are ordinary conditional expectations up to almost-sure equality when the function is integrable and the conditioning σ-algebra is a subalgebra with σ-finite trimmed measure; otherwise the definition returns zero (`Function/ConditionalExpectation/Basic.lean:102`). Here σ(X) means `MeasurableSpace.comap X` of the displayed target measurable space. Measurability of X makes this a subalgebra, and a probability measure gives the relevant finiteness.

`IndepFun` and `iIndepFun` mean independence of the σ-algebras generated by the indicated variables (including all finite subfamilies in the latter). `IdentDistrib` requires almost-everywhere measurability of both functions and equality of their pushforward measures (`Probability/IdentDistrib.lean:71`). `CondIndepFun σ(X) ... U D P` means conditional independence of the generated σ-algebras, using Mathlib's conditional expectation kernel (`Probability/Independence/Conditional.lean:155`); it is not an assumed factorization of arbitrary nonintegrable products. A standard Borel sample space is a measurable space whose σ-algebra is the Borel σ-algebra of some Polish topology (`MeasureTheory/Constructions/Polish/Basic.lean:83`). All stated measurable spaces and their universes are universally quantified.

Convergence `TendstoInMeasure P f atTop (constant c)` means that for every positive error tolerance the P-measure of the set where the distance of fₙ from c is at least that tolerance tends to zero as n→∞ (`Function/ConvergenceInMeasure.lean:57`). For real random variables under a probability measure this is convergence in probability. “Eventually, almost surely” below means ∃n₀ ∀n≥n₀, P(the stated property at n)=1. It is not an assumption merely that these probabilities tend to one.

The sigmoid is s(a)=1/(1+exp(−a)), everywhere strictly between 0 and 1 (`Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Sigmoid.lean:63`). `logOdds(p)=log(p/(1−p))`. For a mean function m, real n,k and predictor η, the defined log likelihood contribution is

    ℓ_m(n,k,η)=k log(m(η))+(n−k) log(1−m(η)).

There is no binomial combinatorial constant. The total binary likelihood is Σᵢℓ_m(nᵢ,kᵢ,(Wθ)ᵢ), and its “residual equation” is the vector with jth entry ΣᵢWᵢⱼ(kᵢ−nᵢm((Wθ)ᵢ)). No derivative assertion is built into that name. The affine coefficient transformation at intercept coordinate 0 is θ′=b e₀+cθ. The SAP helpers `finiteVariance` and `linkScoreMultiplier` are defined but do not occur in any exported theorem proposition.

## Shared definitions for the sample limit theorems

For K controls let Cᵢ=(1,Xᵢ)∈ℝ^{K+1} and Rᵢ=(1,Dᵢ,Xᵢ)∈ℝ^{K+2}. The n-row control and full regression matrices consist of rows Cᵢ and Rᵢ for 0≤i<n. The outcome used in the regression is Yᵢ=Y₀ᵢ+τᵢDᵢ. The actual coefficient estimator is the coordinate 1 of OLS(R,Y). The fitted treatment coefficients are ω̂ₙ=OLS(C,D), and fitted residuals are r̂ₙᵢ=Dᵢ−Cᵢᵀω̂ₙ. The population squared residual is w(s)=(D₀(s)−E[D₀|X₀](s))².

`MomentConvergence(P,F)` means both (a) for every i, Fᵢ is integrable, and (b) n⁻¹Σ_{i<n}Fᵢ converges in probability to E[F₀]. It explicitly assumes a law-of-large-numbers conclusion for that moment sequence; it does not itself assert independence.

`RegressionRegularity(P,D,Y₀,τ,X)` comprises MomentConvergence for all these eight families (every indicated control index is universally quantified):

    Cj Ck; Cj D; D²; D Y₀; Cj Y₀; D² τ; Cj D τ; Cj Ck τ.

In addition det(E[C₀C₀ᵀ])≠0, w is integrable, and E[w]>0. Here “eight” counts D² and DY₀ separately, and the first and last families contain every pair j,k.

`EffectiveMeanRegularity(P,D,Z,X,ω)` sets rᵢ=Dᵢ−Cᵢᵀω and assumes MomentConvergence for all seven families

    Cj Ck; Cj D; r² Z; r²; r Z Cj; r Cj; Z Cj Ck,

as well as det(E[C₀C₀ᵀ])≠0 and E[r₀²]>0.

`EventuallyDefinedRegression` means ∃n₀ ∀n≥n₀, almost surely det(RₙᵀRₙ)≠0. `EventuallyDefinedEffectiveMean` means ∃n₀ ∀n≥n₀, almost surely det(CₙᵀCₙ)≠0 and Σ_{i<n}r̂ₙᵢ²>0. These assumptions concern each sufficiently large finite n, with a deterministic threshold. The formulas are total even at n=0 or a singular sample, but these premises exclude reliance on such values in the tail.
