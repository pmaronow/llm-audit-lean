## Shared notation, fully expanded

The following notation is used explicitly in the five readings below.

* A response is a tuple `(X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1)`, with covariate in the stated type,
  `D0,D1 : Bool`, and `Y0,Y1 : ℝ`. Write `τ=Y1-Y0`, `C={D0=false,D1=true}`,
  and `F={D0=true,D1=false}`. For assignment `z : Bool`, received treatment is
  `D(z)=D1` if `z=true`, and `D0` otherwise; outcome is `Y(z)=Y1` if `D(z)=true`,
  and `Y0` otherwise. Boolean indicators take the real values zero and one.
  `assignResponse` just ignores the assignment coordinate of a product.
* On a finite type `L`, a law is a real function `p` with `p(i)≥0` for every i
  and `Σ_i p(i)=1`. Write `P_p(A)=Σ_i (if A(i) then p(i) else 0)`,
  `E_p(v)=Σ_i p(i)v(i)`, and
  `M_p(v;A)=Σ_i p(i)(if A(i) then v(i) else 0)/P_p(A)`.
  These are exactly `IsFiniteLaw`, `finiteProbability`, `finiteExpectation`,
  and `finiteConditionalMean`.
* Finite independence of maps V,W means **for every value a,b**,
  `P_p(V=a,W=b)=P_p(V=a)P_p(W=b)`. Finite conditional independence given X
  means **for every x,a,b with P_p(X=x)>0**,
  `P_p(V=a,W=b,X=x)/P_p(X=x)` equals
  `[P_p(V=a,X=x)/P_p(X=x)][P_p(W=b,X=x)/P_p(X=x)]`.
* Finite IV conditions are: p is a law; `0<P_p(Z=true)<1`; the **entire** tuple
  `(X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1)` is independent of Z in the preceding atomwise sense;
  `P_p(F)=0`; and `M_p(1_D;Z=true)-M_p(1_D;Z=false)≠0`.
  Exclusion is built into the response/outcome definition.
* Write `s_p(x)=P_p(C,X=x)/P_p(X=x)`. Finite latent ignorability means
  **for every x with P_p(X=x)>0**,
  `M_p(τ;X=x)=M_p(τ;C,X=x)`. Finite receipt ignorability at x is the conjunction,
  for j=0,1, of `M_p(Yj;D=true,X=x)=M_p(Yj;D=false,X=x)`.
  These are mean equalities, not distributional independence.
* Define `A_p(w,v,z)=M_p(wv;Z=z)/M_p(w;Z=z)` and
  `Δ_p(w,v)=A_p(w,v,true)-A_p(w,v,false)`.
  Then `finiteWald=Δ_p(w,Y(Z))/Δ_p(w,1_D)`, and `finiteArmDifference=Δ_p`.
* For a measure μ, `P(A)=(μ A).toReal` and `M(v;A)=(∫_A v dμ)/P(A)`.
  Integrals here are Mathlib Bochner integrals into ℝ. For a probability measure
  and measurable A, these have the usual probability/event-mean meanings.
  Write `s(x)=P(C,X=x)/P(X=x)` and `τc(x)=M(τ;C,X=x)`.
  `LatentMeanEquality` at x is `M(τ;X=x)=M(τ;C,X=x)`.
  `ReceiptMeanEquality` at x is the two equalities
  `M(Yj;D=true,X=x)=M(Yj;D=false,X=x)` for j=0,1.
* The population weighted arm mean is `M(wv;Z=z)/M(w;Z=z)`.
  `populationWald` is its outcome difference divided by its treatment-indicator
  difference. Write this ratio as `W(w)`.
  `populationCovariance(u,v)=∫uv dμ-(∫u dμ)(∫v dμ)`.
* A randomized core model requires measurability of X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1,Z,
  integrability of Y0,Y1, Mathlib `IndepFun` between Z and the full response tuple,
  and `0<P(Z=true)<1`. `IndepFun` is independence of the measurable σ-algebras
  generated by the maps (equivalently factorization on measurable preimages).
