## Expanded notation and assumptions

A potential unit is a tuple `(X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1)`, where `D0,D1` are Booleans and
`Y0,Y1` are real. Write `tau=Y1-Y0`, `C={D0=0,D1=1}`, and
`F={D0=1,D1=0}`. Boolean `1` means `true`, and `bit` embeds it in the reals.
For Boolean assignment `Z`, received treatment is `D=D1` if `Z=1`, otherwise
`D=D0`; the observed outcome is `Y=Y1` if `D=1`, otherwise `Y=Y0`.
Thus exclusion and this consistency rule are built into the objects.

For a finite type `L`, `ValidLaw(p)` means every `p(l)>=0` and
`sum_l p(l)=1`. Finite expectation and probability are respectively
`sum_l p(l)v(l)` and `sum_{A(l)}p(l)`. Finite conditional expectation is
`sum_{A(l)}p(l)v(l)/P(A)`. `FIndep(p,V,W)` means, for every pair of values
`v,w`, `P(V=v,W=w)=P(V=v)P(W=w)`. `randomized(p,e)` gives mass
`p(l)e` at `(l,1)` and `p(l)(1-e)` at `(l,0)`.

`FIV(p,U,Z)` means, in order: valid finite law; `0<P(Z=1)<1`;
independence of `(X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1)` and `Z`; `P(F)=0`; and the nonzero
first stage `E[D|Z=1]-E[D|Z=0] != 0`. Its finite compliance score is
`P(C,X=x)/P(X=x)`. Finite latent ignorability means, for every `x` with
`P(X=x)>0`, `E[tau|X=x]=E[tau|C,X=x]`. Finite typical ignorability at
`x` means both `E[Yd|D=1,X=x]=E[Yd|D=0,X=x]`, for `d=0,1`.

On a measurable space with measure `mu`, `mP(A)=(mu A).toReal`, and
`mCE(v,A)=(integral_A v dmu)/mP(A)`. All measure-space theorem blocks
assume `mu` is a probability measure. Accordingly `toReal` never converts
an infinite mass into a spurious finite probability. Integrals are Mathlib
Bochner integrals; they are defined to be zero for nonintegrable inputs.
Real division is total, with `a/0=0`. Such totalized expressions must not
automatically be read as ordinary conditional means at null events.

`IVAssumptions(mu,U,Z)` means, in order: measurability of `X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1,Z`;
integrability of `Y0,Y1`; Mathlib independence of `Z` and the full tuple
`(X,D0,D1,Y0,Y1)`; `0<P(Z=1)<1`; `mu(F)=0`; and the nonzero conditional
receipt first stage. Independence is independence of the sigma algebras
generated by the displayed random functions (factorization on measurable
events). No conditional independence given `X` substitutes for the stated
unconditional independence.

`latentAt(x)` is `E[tau|X=x]=E[tau|C,X=x]`, using the event ratios above.
`typicalAt(x)` is equality of received-treatment conditional means of each
potential outcome. Write `c_atom(x)=P(C,X=x)/P(X=x)` (`scoreAt`) and
`m_atom(x)=E[tau|C,X=x]` (`complierAt`). These remain totalized at null
profiles unless hypotheses explicitly exclude them.

For weights `w`, define `A_z(w,V)=E[wV|Z=z]/E[w|Z=z]` and
`W(w)=(A_1(w,Y)-A_0(w,Y))/(A_1(w,D)-A_0(w,D))`. These are `mArm` and
`mWald`, with the analogous finite definition `fWald`.
For a sample indexed by `Fin N`, define
`a_z(w,V)=sum_{i:Zi=z} wi Vi / sum_{i:Zi=z}wi`,
`W_N(w)=(a_1(w,Y)-a_0(w,Y))/(a_1(w,D)-a_0(w,D))`, and
`cross(Z,V)=sum_i (Zi-mean(Z))(Vi-mean(V))`.
`SampleDefined(w,D,Z)` requires both arm weight sums and the weighted
receipt first stage to be nonzero.

`ScoreVersions(mu,U,c,m)` requires `c,m:X->R` measurable and, almost surely,
`c(X)=E[1_C|sigma(X)]` and `c(X)m(X)=E[1_C tau|sigma(X)]`.
`A1Versions(mu,U,m)` means `m(X)=E[tau|sigma(X)]` almost surely.
Here `sigma(X)` is exactly the comap of the measurable space on `X` along
the covariate map. Mathlib's conditional expectation defaults to zero if
its sigma algebra is not a subalgebra, its trimmed measure is not sigma
finite, or its input is not integrable. The IV measurability and probability
assumptions, and integrability of the indicated outcomes, remove these
defaults in the uses here.

For an infinite sample, `ICSWConditions(mu,U,Z,c,m,s)` means ALL of:

1. For every natural `i`, `IVAssumptions(mu,U_i,Z_i)`.
2. The functions `((X_i,D0_i,D1_i,Y0_i,Y1_i),Z_i)` form an independent
   family (every finite subfamily is independent).
3. For every `i`, that joint function is identically distributed with the
   joint function at `i=0`; Mathlib `IdentDistrib` includes almost everywhere
   measurability and equality of pushforward measures.
4. For every `i`, `ScoreVersions(mu,U_i,c,m)`.
5. There exists a real `epsilon>0` such that, for every `i`, almost surely
   `epsilon<c(X_i)<=1`.
6. For every natural `n,i`, `s(n,i):Omega->R` is measurable.
7. For every natural `n`, almost surely, for every `i` in `Fin(n+1)`,
   `0<s(n,i)<=1`.
8. `max_{0<=i<=n}|s(n,i)-c(X_i)|` converges in measure to zero.

Write `I_n=W_{n+1}(1/s(n,i))` (`icswSequence`) and
`I^w_n=W_{n+1}(1/max(s(n,i),cutoff(n)))` (`winsorizedSequence`).
`RatiosEventuallyDefined(w)` means: for almost every draw `omega`, there
exists a threshold (depending on `omega`) beyond which every sample
`SampleDefined(w(n,i,omega),D_i,Z_i)` holds. This is stronger than merely
requiring that the probability of undefined samples tends to zero.

Mathlib `TendstoInMeasure(mu,f,atTop,g)` says, for every positive extended
distance threshold, the measure of `{omega: threshold<=edist(f_n,g)}` tends
to zero. Here the measure is a probability measure and the functions are
real valued, so this is convergence in probability. `Tendsto` of real
sequences at `atTop` is the usual numerical limit; `forall^ae` is almost
everywhere; `forall^eventually n` is for all sufficiently large naturals.

For a real vector `s` of length `N=n+1`, `Type7Quantile(s,lambda,q)` means
there exists a permutation `pi` sorting `s` in nondecreasing order, followed
by an index `j` with `j<=n lambda<j+1`, such that
`q=(1-(n lambda-j))s_(j)+(n lambda-j)s_(min(j+1,n))` (zero-based ranks).
This is the linear interpolation convention on levels `[0,1]`; the next
index is clamped at the last order statistic. `belowCount(s,q)` counts
strict inequalities `s_i<q`. `weightChange(s,q)=1/s-1/max(s,q)`.

Other definitions: `B(p,d)=p^d(1-p)^(1-d)` for Boolean `d`;
`nestedReceipt(a,b,z)=a(1-b)z+ab`; `positiveScore(r1,r0)=max(r1-r0,0)`.
`binomialMass(n,p,k)=choose(n,k)p^k(1-p)^(n-k)` for `0<=k<=n`; hence its
natural subtraction is not truncated in any unintended way.
`binomialArmLaw` is the product of two such masses.

The six-point law uses a single covariate value, type masses
`(C,A,N)=(1/2,1/4,1/4)`, and independent fair assignment. `constantUnit`
has `(Y0,Y1)=(0,1)` in every type. `case2Unit` has `(Y0,Y1)=(1,2)` for
always takers and `(0,1)` for compliers and never takers. `liftUnit` ignores
the assignment coordinate. The unused baseline indices are
`2+a+b*x1+c*x2+d*x3+epsilon` and `-2+e+f*x1+g*x2+h*x3+epsilon`;
the unused nonlinear indices are
`2+a+(1+b*x1)(1+c*x2)+d*x3^3/3+ell` and
`-2+e+(1+f*x1)(1+g*x2)+h*x3^3/3+ell`.
