## Shared expansion and conventions

A finite type `ι` is the population. A treatment set `s` has `M=|s|`, control complement `K=|sᶜ|`, and `N=|ι|`. `Admissible s` means `M,K≥2`. `treatment` is its 0/1 indicator; `observed s y1 y0` picks the appropriate fixed potential outcome. `groupMean` divides the sum on a set by its cardinality. `popVariance` and `popCovariance` are centered finite sums divided by `N`, rather than `N−1`. `estimate` is treated mean minus control mean, `intercept` the control mean, `residual` the ordinary intercept-and-treatment residual. The two-column design has rows `(1,X_i)`. `gramInverse` is the explicit inverse with entries `1/K,-1/K,1/M+1/K` under nonempty arms; leverage and coefficient weight contract the design and its inverse. `sampleVariance` divides centered within-arm squares by arm size minus one. Neyman is treated sample variance/M plus control sample variance/K. HR, HC2, HC3 are the corresponding squared-coefficient-weight residual sums, with HC2 denominator `1−leverage` and HC3 its square. HC1 multiplies HR by `N/(N−2)`. `ols` is pooled residual squares/(N−2) times the treatment diagonal of the explicit inverse. `imputed0=Y−X*estimate`, `imputed1=Y+(1−X)*estimate`. `constantEffects` inserts these into `varianceExpression`, whose formula is `N/(N−1)*(S1²/M+S0²/K)+(2Cov−S1²−S0²)/(N−1)`, with all population moments divisor `N`. `expectation M` averages over every size-M treatment set with equal mass, and `randomizationVariance` is the corresponding mean squared deviation of the difference-in-means estimate from its assignment mean. These are the meanings of the shared closure declarations in Part1.

The `Population` structure is an `N,M` and two real potential-outcome vectors with `M≥2` and `M<N−1`, hence `K≥2`. All estimator abbreviations use the observed vector under each assignment. `eventProbability` is a uniform finite average of indicators. `GrowingPopulations` means `N_n→∞`; `StandardizedNormalLimit` asserts eventual positive actual variance and pointwise CDF convergence of `(estimate−population effect)/sqrt(true variance)` to the standard normal CDF. `ConvergesInProbability P f c` requires every positive epsilon to have assignment probability of `epsilon<|f−c|` tend to zero. `FirstOrderEquivalent A B` uses the relative event `epsilon*|B|<|A−B|`, including a zero `B`. `coverage` is assignment probability of `|estimate−effect|≤z*sqrt(V)`; `ConservativeCoverage` requires, for every positive epsilon, eventual coverage at least `2 Phi(z)−1−epsilon`. `UnbalancedVarianceLimit P a v` requires growth, every row `N≠2M`, `v>0`, `N*trueVariance→v`, and `N*OLS` and `N*CE` both converge in assignment probability to `a`.
