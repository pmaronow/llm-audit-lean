# Reading the formal results

- **Target:** an identified claim selected for formalization.
- **Whole:** records complete coverage of the selected formal target, which may itself be a counterexample.
- **Restricted:** records a narrower statement or added assumptions; the guide gives the restriction.
- **Refuted:** records a counterexample to the selected assertion or reading.
- **Omitted:** has no assigned current theorem; its explanation remains available.
- **Challenge:** the formal statement, with an intentional proof placeholder.
- **Solution:** the proof of that same statement.
- **Definitions:** the mathematical objects and predicates used by the statement.

Outcome labels reproduce the certified release's classifications. Current scope notes qualify correspondence to the published text, including restrictions missed by a recorded classification. A proof establishes the Lean statement under its hypotheses. It does not establish that the statement covers an entire paper, or that its interpretation of the paper is correct.
