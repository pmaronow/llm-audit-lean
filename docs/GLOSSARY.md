# Reading the formal results

- **Target:** an identified claim selected for formalization.
- **Whole:** covers the identified claim under its stated assumptions.
- **Restricted:** proves a narrower statement or adds assumptions; the guide gives the restriction.
- **Refuted:** supplies a counterexample to the precise formalized claim.
- **Omitted:** has no current theorem; its explanation remains available.
- **Challenge:** the formal statement, with an intentional proof placeholder.
- **Solution:** the proof of that same statement.
- **Definitions:** the mathematical objects and predicates used by the statement.

A proof establishes the Lean statement under its hypotheses. It does not establish that the statement covers an entire paper, or that its interpretation of the paper is correct.
