**P003-T8 (restricted).** Uses a real critical value z in the displayed rational enclosure; it does not identify z with the normal 0.975 quantile or prove that quantile enclosure. Omits the extensions to every sqrt(2)<=z<=2 and every z>=1.95, the pointwise variance lower bound V_k>=1/50, the sharper pointwise HC1 width bound 2*z*sqrt(1/50), and the sharper pointwise HC0 width bound 2*z*sqrt(12/625). Retains both exact coverage fractions, both design medians, the HC0 classification and 0.54 bound, and the finite-run sharp median bound and reported-pair incompatibility for the enclosed critical values. The statement uses the explicit two-type Wald formulas without a separate labeled-assignment-to-k derivation. The design conclusions certify the displayed values as medians without asserting uniqueness.
