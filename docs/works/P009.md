**P009-T11 (restricted).** Only the failure of guaranteed eventual largest-in-absolute-value dominance is asserted, for sequences with uniform q>=4 regularity, independent Bernoulli assignment, restricted interference, and d_AVG/C1 tending to infinity. The specified square-size m-block fair-Bernoulli witness and its full outcome schedule are not stated. Omitted witness clauses are: the full-cube [0,2] bounds and regularity at every moment order; the exact interference and dependence graphs, C1=2, d_AVG=m=sqrt(n), the ratio m/2 and its divergence, and Assumption 2 for that family; the expected direct effects all equal to 1, tau_MSQ=1 and tau_MSQ/d_AVG=1/m; the xi and weighted-xi formulas, reciprocal products and B1=2/m^2>0; the arm-independent conditional covariances, their alternating-sum cancellations and B2=0; the comparison 0=B2<B1<=tau_MSQ/d_AVG with equality of the last two terms exactly at m=2; and the extension to every n>=4 with its floor-defined blocks, isolated units, and exact C1, d_AVG, tau_MSQ, B1 and B2 formulas. The printed big-O bounds and Proposition 7 are not refuted.

**P009-T21 (refuted).** Retains the feasible full-support sampling-model refutation of the exact B2 evaluation and the correct aggregate formulas. Drops the six individual pair covariance values, separate BER and HT-variance evaluations, and the numerical c0=-1/4 specialization. The paper's c0=-1/4 hypothetical is outside the uniform-baseline support and is only an algebraic auxiliary; this theorem makes no assertion refuting its printed convergence display.

**P009-T23 (refuted).** Retains the uniform-baseline sampling integrals of both signed biases, both design variances, both mean squared errors, and the complete-design squared-bias/variance ratio. Adds integrability of each of the twelve sampling integrands. Drops the universal fixed-baseline clauses (a)–(d), the supportwise balance/outcome/effect/estimator/marginal identities, the claimed divergence of the squared-bias/variance ratios, and any identification of the integrals of design variances with a variance on a joint sampling-and-assignment space. The auxiliary 100-unit zero-baseline row is not used.

<a id="p009-t24"></a>
**P009-T24 (restricted).** Retains the finite complete-interference exception for every positive even sample size, arbitrary fixed baselines, and both balanced complete and paired designs, including supportwise identities, both signed and absolute biases, and both mean squared errors. Drops the blanket empirical assertion that bias is negligible in all other settings and sample sizes, and does not certify the printed Monte Carlo table entries.

Does not assert that the deposited code computes this formula, that the four published captions use the corrected wording, or that the particular 50,000-draw table entries have the stated finite values.
