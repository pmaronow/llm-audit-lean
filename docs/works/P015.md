**P015-T4 (restricted).** Retains formal clauses (i)-(iii), the multivalued-support and fitted-zero counterexamples, and the rare-overlap positivity and arbitrarily-small-effective-mass family for every 0 < epsilon < 1/2. Estimator unbiasedness, moments, deviation bounds, and consistency are asserted only at epsilon = 1/1000000, rather than jointly with arbitrarily small effective-profile mass. Omits the empirical Jensen country-contribution assertion.

<a id="p015-t7"></a>
**P015-T7 (restricted).** Retains all specified finite-law, causal-model, moment, score, maximizing-branch, OLS and same-mean derivative facts. The square-link family is fitted as a working model without requiring correct conditional-mean specification. At a = 1/16 and epsilon = 0, the true probabilities at X = -1, 0, 1 are 5/16, 1/8, 5/16, while the likelihood maximizer fits 1/4. Omits a refutation of Appendix C's first-order scaling assertion under its correctly specified conditional-probability model reading; the derivative discrepancy concerns population expected-likelihood projections allowing misspecification.

**P015-T8 (whole).** The square-link illustration permits misspecification of the working conditional-mean model.
