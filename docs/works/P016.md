**P016-T5 (omitted).** The target is the printed information and reference-distribution comparison. No correction replacing it with a fixed-margin existential claim has been accepted. The available T2 margin-invariance theorem negates that different existential claim, but does not refute the printed comparison: equality of full group margins is stronger than equality of the scalar KS, as the T2 scalar-KS witness shows. T1 pairing/design witnesses and T2 design identities establish their own corrected claims without proving equivalence to the printed prose. The proposed supporting refutation addresses the different fixed-margin claim and is not used as a theorem for T5; its content is already implied by T2.

**P016-T6 (restricted).** This states only the algebraic consistency consequence of the raw-distance display. It imposes no affine-normalization requirement on the arbitrary map g and omits the printed description that the indirect effect is linear in the raw distance sums. Unit variance is restricted to across-unit sample variance with divisor n-1 on every design assignment. Omits other unit-variance conventions, identification with the complete SSR + Design regression and testing procedure, and the empirical rejection proportions reported for the simulations and figures.
