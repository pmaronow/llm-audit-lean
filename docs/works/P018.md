# P018: Cluster-Robust Variance Estimation for Dyadic Data

[All works](README.md) · [Machine-readable detail](../../catalog/works/P018.json)

P. M. Aronow, Cyrus Samii, and Valentina A. Assenova (2015). Cluster-Robust Variance Estimation for Dyadic Data. Political Analysis 23(4):564–577.

DOI: [10.1093/pan/mpv018](https://doi.org/10.1093/pan/mpv018)

arXiv: [1312.3398](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.3398)

## Overview

This paper studies sandwich variance estimation for regressions on dyads that can be dependent when they share a unit. The Lean development proves a corrected probability-limit argument for variance consistency and a projection central limit theorem with studentized inference for positive-variance contrasts. Exact counterexamples address the printed conditional variance identity, finite-sample negative variance estimates, weighted-design assumptions, inverse-moment arguments, and the covariance correspondence of the fitted random-effects benchmark; algebraic corrections also cover the logistic sandwich.

Areas: Regression and variance estimation, Asymptotic statistics.

## Key results

For bounded symmetric measurable dyad data generated from iid units, with an intercept and nonsingular population Gram matrix, the corrected result gives $`N\hat V-V_N\to0`$ in probability for the unconditional score-covariance target $`V_N`$.

## Corrections and counterexamples

The universal reading of printed Lemma 1 is false under its bounded iid-unit and rank assumptions because its exact conditional-covariance representation of the unconditional score variance can fail.

[`AronowAudit.P018.Wave1.lemma1_printed_refuted`](../../AronowAudit/P018/Wave1/PrintedLemma1.lean#L15) · [P018-T26](#p018-t26)

The printed exact correspondence between the simulation covariance and the independently fitted two-role random-effects family fails: a three-unit shared-effect covariance has no representation in that family with nonnegative variance components.

[`AronowAudit.P018.Wave1.benchmark_correspondence_printed_false`](../../AronowAudit/P018/Wave1/Gap/DecideC.lean#L755) · [P018-T25](#p018-t25)

The instruction to obtain standard errors by taking diagonal square roots is not always defined: an explicit full-rank six-dyad OLS sample has variance-estimator diagonals $`-17/50`$ and $`-58/625`$.

[`AronowAudit.P018.negative_ols_certificate`](../../AronowAudit/P018/P018_Audit.lean#L454) · [P018-T17](#p018-t17)

Where Appendix E repeats the first dyad's regressor in the cross term, the corrected logistic score product uses $`r_dr_{d'}X_dX_{d'}'`$; for finite distinct two-unit dyads, binary outcomes and nonsingular bread, Lean proves the incidence decomposition and symmetry of the resulting sandwich.

## Caveats

- The principal corrected asymptotic results use all unordered non-self dyads from iid unit characteristics, a symmetric measurable kernel, a common almost-sure finite bound on regressors and response, an intercept, and a nonsingular population Gram matrix; they do not cover arbitrary dyadic dependence.
- Finite singular samples use a zero inverse convention; their probabilities vanish under the population-rank assumption, and convergence is expressed with an entrywise matrix norm.
- Variance consistency allows a degenerate leading covariance; conventional studentized inference additionally requires strictly positive asymptotic variance for the tested contrast, making the nonpositive estimated-variance event asymptotically negligible under the formal pivot convention.
- Probability limits and a CLT do not establish convergence of finite-sample variances or expectations of inverse Gram matrices; the separate uniform-integrability result assumes its tail condition rather than establishing it for every paper estimator.
- The random-effects counterexample concerns independent first-role and second-role effects in the fitted benchmark; a genuinely shared unit-effect covariance model is not refuted, and no empirical efficiency claim follows.
- The infinite-moment result certifies the repeated-record construction and its limits; its directed-record reformulation is outside that selected declaration, and the finite negative-OLS certificate alone is not a population or asymptotic theorem.
