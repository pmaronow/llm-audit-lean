# P023: A Note on Close Elections and Regression Analysis of the Party Incumbency Advantage

[All works](README.md) · [Machine-readable detail](../../catalog/works/P023.json)

P. M. Aronow, David R. Mayhew, and Winston Lin (2014). A Note on Close Elections and Regression Analysis of the Party Incumbency Advantage. Statistics, Politics and Policy 5(1–2):1–11.

DOI: [10.1515/spp-2014-0003](https://doi.org/10.1515/spp-2014-0003)

## Overview

The work relates regressions of party incumbency outcomes on election margins to close-election regression discontinuity estimates. The Lean development proves the exact coarsened-regression identity of Proposition 1, derives residual-weighted polynomial coefficient error bounds, and proves a corrected version of Proposition 2 under explicit quantitative assumptions. It also gives finite examples of distant-outcome dependence and a population example showing that continuity alone need not make polynomial coefficients approach the cutoff jump.

Areas: Regression and variance estimation, Asymptotic statistics, Causal identification.

## Key results

For two-party election data, bandwidth $`0\lt k\lt 1`$, and an election strictly on each side of the cutoff inside the window, every OLS solution in the printed intercept, party-win, and bin-indicator design has party-win coefficient equal to the rectangular-window difference in means.

[`AronowAudit.P023.printed_coarsened_proposition1`](../../AronowAudit/P023/P023_Audit.lean#L1350) · [P023-T11](#p023-t11)

For margins $`X`$ supported on $`[-1,1]`$, conditional mean $`E[Y\mid X]=\alpha+\beta I[X\ge0]+h(X)`$, finite outcome second moment, bounded measurable $`h`$, and degree $`J\ge1`$ with a positive definite regression moment matrix, the population party-win coefficient $`b_J`$ satisfies $`b_J-\beta=E[r_J(h-p)]/v_J`$ and $`|b_J-\beta|`$ is bounded by both $`E|r_J|\,\|h-p\|_\infty/v_J`$ and $`\|h-p\|_2/\sqrt{v_J}`$ for every polynomial $`p`$ of degree at most $`J`$, where $`v_J=E[r_J^2]`$.

[`AronowAudit.P023.Wave1.corrected_population_bias_proof`](../../AronowAudit/P023/Wave1/Gap/GLP023B3.lean#L409) · [P023-T7](#p023-t7)

For uniform margins on $`[-1,1]`$, there exists a continuous $`h`$ with $`h(0)=0`$ and $`|h|\le1/12`$ such that the population polynomial coefficients for mean $`1/2+h`$ diverge to positive infinity along increasing degrees, while the shrinking-window population RD contrast tends to zero.

## Corrections and counterexamples

The printed sole-reliance-on-close-elections claim extends to polynomial controls; Lean proves a full-rank sixteen-election degree-7 example in which changing one distant outcome by $`1/100`$ changes the polynomial coefficient by $`7/6428`$ while leaving close-window outcomes, RD, and coarsened-regression coefficients unchanged.

[`AronowAudit.P023.degree7_counterexample`](../../AronowAudit/P023/P023_Audit.lean#L973) · [P023-T2](#p023-t2)

Printed Proposition 2 asserts that polynomial OLS approximates shrinking-window RD under weak regularity and sufficiently large degree. Under the explicit Lipschitz, local-law, moment and iid sampling conditions, with $`k_N\gt 0`$, $`k_N\to0`$ and $`Nk_N\to\infty`$, the corrected theorem gives an $`O(J^{-1/2})`$ population coefficient error bound and convergence in probability of polynomial OLS minus RD to zero when $`J_N\to\infty`$ and $`(J_N+2)^2/(N\lambda_{J_N}^3)\to0`$, where $`\lambda_J`$ is the smallest regression moment eigenvalue.

## Caveats

- The exact coarsened identity quantifies over every normal-equation solution, including cases with nonunique nuisance coefficients from empty bins; ties at zero may carry either binary party indicator.
- The polynomial error theorem assumes margins supported on $`[-1,1]`$, conditional mean $`\alpha+\beta I[X\ge0]+h(X)`$, and a positive definite moment matrix; its selected declaration covers the population clauses, with separate finite-sample statements. Here $`r_J`$ is the residual of the party-win indicator after projection on $`1,X,\ldots,X^J`$.
- The corrected consistency theorem assumes a fixed margin law supported on $`[-1,1]`$, no atom at zero, domination of a positive multiple of Lebesgue measure near zero, $`h`$ Lipschitz on $`[-1,1]`$ with $`h(0)=0`$, finite outcome second moment, and bounded local conditional variance; bandwidths satisfy $`k_N\gt 0`$, $`k_N\to0`$ and $`Nk_N\to\infty`$.
- Fixed-degree OLS converges to its population coefficient, which may differ from the cutoff jump; simultaneous equivalence with RD requires the explicit degree and smallest-eigenvalue rate condition.
- The continuity counterexample concerns population coefficients for one fixed uniform margin law and does not by itself negate the full sampling proposition; its bounded continuous witness is existential and is not Lipschitz.
- Nonzero distant-outcome weights do not preclude convergence of both estimators to a common cutoff target; the statistical statements do not establish a causal interpretation of that target or verify the empirical table.
