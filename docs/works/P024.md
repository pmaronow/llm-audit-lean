<a id="p024-t4"></a>

<a id="p024-t6"></a>
**P024-T6 (restricted).** Require either a treatment count m fixed almost surely, 0<m<N, with every actual unit marginal m/N, or an assignment law on proper nonempty subsets uniform conditional on its treatment count. This includes complete individual randomization and independent common-p individual assignment conditioned on nonempty arms. Arbitrary equal-marginal dependent laws with varying counts are not covered.

<a id="p024-t7"></a>
<a id="p024-t8"></a>
**P024-T8 (restricted).** Assume a predetermined interior treatment quota m_b in every block on every assignment and actual common within-block treatment probabilities m_b/N_b. Establish the population-share weighted raw-mean identity and unbiasedness under these conditions; no result for variable block treatment counts or unequal within-block propensities is asserted. Uniform subsets and independence across blocks are not required.
