**P029-T8 (restricted).** Retains the weighted-objective rules, strict concavity, unique general free-cell optimum, three concrete corrected free-cell fits, constant-positive-weight equivalence and the numerical Wald evaluations. Does not identify free-cell optima with finite-coefficient one-sided or nested two-sided probit fits. <a id="p029-t9"></a>
**P029-T9 (restricted).** Retains the prescribed 18,933-record completions, exported pooled counts and both directions of the strict sign reversal, preservation of the binary labels and arbitrary compatible raw codes, arbitrary outcomes, and assignment-vector support. Omits the standalone general binary-count sign identity and independent staged-data summaries: the 4,660 Raleigh records, 8,721 assignments, 1,685 Raleigh assignments, the 602/1370 and 160/315 Raleigh rates, and the 21 unknown-code records and their breakdown. Does not validate a raw data file.

**P029-T10 (restricted).** Retains the finite count-sign formulas, both Raleigh rational rate calculations, the binary-label-compatible completions and the covariance decomposition. Omits empirical assertions about prior voting, fitted compliance scores including the within-Raleigh comparison, the quantitative between-city contribution, party/race availability and zero coding, and other covariate missingness and imputation. Does not assert raw-string-code preservation here. Adds positive probability for the distinguished group in its conditional covariance formula. In part (b), only the two displayed rational rate calculations are retained; the empirical identification of seven treatment-assigned unknown-code records, five of them contacted, and the exclusion producing 155/308 are not certified.

**P029-T21 (refuted).** The two false joint implications are refuted. The additional true constant-effect component is stated with measurable conditional-mean versions and uniform positive overlap, and only positive-probability, positive-score profiles are evaluated. The weaker positivity-only constant-effect assertion and sample consistency are not asserted. The marginal refutation uses independence of the complier indicator, not a separately stated full-type marginal implication.

<a id="p029-t22"></a>
**P029-T22 (restricted).** Records only the empirical RMSE scoring convention for a supplied array of 2500 estimates and a supplied constant benchmark of 5. Omits the simulation design and estimator implementations, including alpha=0.275; any connection to historical estimates or simulated datasets; identification of the sample average effect, population ATE, or LATE with 5; and every numerical entry and graphical result of Table A3 and Figure A3. No simulation output or implementation is validated or refuted.

**P029-T26 (restricted).** Retains the general population weighted-moment identities and ATE consistency. Adds maximum-in-sample fitted-score consistency, measurable positive estimates in (0,1], iid joint draws, integrable potential outcomes, measurable conditional-mean versions, second-countable Borel covariates, and explicit support overlap and latent-ignorability conditions. Also requires the sample ratios to be eventually defined along almost every draw sequence. Does not derive the result from the paper's pointwise Assumption 2 alone. The sample conclusion states only consistency of the final Wald ratio; it omits the Appendix's two separate convergence assertions for the sample weighted outcome-arm difference and the sample weighted receipt-arm difference.

**P029-T27 (refuted).** The counterexample refutes only the blanket claim that failure of Assumption 1 must cause bias. It does not refute the integral-limit or constant-score identities.
