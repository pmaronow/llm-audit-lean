## Scope and restrictions

<a id="p042-t1"></a>
<a id="p042-t2"></a>
**P042-T2 (restricted).** Retains general mathematical clauses (i)-(viii) and the covariance-based and printed half-width identities. Omits application to actual historical contrasts and pools, estimation of their covariance matrices, numerical pool and mail-comparison recalculations, the staged-size recalculation, block/overlap-specific numerical bounds, relabeling the printed inference, and the requested rejection of the old GGL-only covariance certification. Finite second moments, positive marginal variances, positive arm sizes and feasible nonempty randomization designs rule out undefined statistical quantities.
