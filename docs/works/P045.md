**P045-T4 (restricted).** Retains mathematical parts (a)-(d), including the exact bias, nonnegativity, equality if and only if within-group effects are constant, both general singleton-drop biases, both dilated biases and their strict negativity criterion, and undefined corrected toy variance on every admissible toy assignment. Omits execution of an R implementation that propagates undefined arm variances to NA and emits a warning, and replacement of the reported toy output entries by NA. The optional regenerated six-by-six toy is not asserted.

**P045-T6 (restricted).** Retains mathematical event characterizations and all corrected/complement expectation formulas, including threshold sufficiency. Omits the cited numerical two-unit bias instance, literal execution of the repaired R code, recomputation of software weights, and outcome-generation or population-size changes. The general event identity is stated for a genuine finite design with positive event probability; the population contrast requires a positive population size and positive probabilities for both extreme events.
