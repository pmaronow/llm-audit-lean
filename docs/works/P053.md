**P053-T6 (restricted).** Retains the local variance difference and ratio conclusions, both constant-effect variance approximations, and the sharp-null normal Wald limit and nominal rejection probability. Omits the Introduction's equivalence of the FRT and Wald tests under local alternatives (conditional FRT distributions, quantiles, p-values and rejection rules). Additionally, variance-ratio and studentized conclusions require eventual positivity of the OLS variance at every supported assignment; no such condition is added to the unstudentized variance conclusions.
