**P061-T10 (restricted).** Retains all three corrected rate characterizations as one conjunction of Mathlib little-o and big-O equivalences for nonnegative real sequences indexed by population size. It identifies no dependency graph and does not assert that any particular sequence satisfies these conditions, prove a CLT or Chebyshev sharpness, or verify the literature attributions. The finite quotient bound is quantified only at positive population sizes; asymptotic assertions disregard the initial zero index.

<a id="p061-t11"></a>
**P061-T11 (restricted).** Adds finiteness of the positive-probability assignment support. The feasible assignment type remains arbitrary and may contain infinitely many zero-mass assignments; no stability, independence or global outcome bound is assumed. All infinite-positive-support designs are excluded, including those with existing targeted moments. Retains the supported-atom exposure-probability formula, finite targeted absolute moments, conditional exposure-mean equalities, AEPO averaging, unit conditional-mean contrasts, and both AEED identities.
